Getting here

Walk over from BART

Exit the West Dublin/Pleasanton BART station and walk north over the pedestrian overpass. Follow the signs for Dublin Boulevard.

The office is in the back corner of a shopping center, behind Mancini’s Sleepworld. It sits on Dublin Boulevard between Golden Gate Street and Regional Street.

Next door is a larger center with H Mart, Ross, Michaels, and DSW.
