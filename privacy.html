Privacy and accessibility

How this site handles your information, and how to reach us if anything is hard to use.

Your health information

The practice’s HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices explains how your health information is used and protected. Read the HIPAA Policy Notice (PDF).

Patient forms

Patient forms open on newpatientinfo.net, a separate secure service. Information you enter there goes to that service, not to this website.

The concierge chat

In this preview, the Book or ask chat runs only in your browser. Nothing you type is sent or stored. Please never share health details in a chat.

Voice input uses your browser’s speech feature. Some browsers send that audio to their own speech service.

Maps and cookies

The map on the Contact page loads from Google only when you choose to show it. Google’s privacy policy applies once it loads.

This preview sets no tracking or advertising cookies. It remembers which homepage photo you saw last, in your browser only, so the next visit shows a different one. [to confirm: analytics plans for the live site]

Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use this site. It is built to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.2 at level AA.

  • Every page works with a keyboard and has a skip link.
  • Images have text descriptions.
  • Motion is reduced when your device asks for it.
  • Text and buttons meet AA color contrast.

If anything is hard to use, call (925) 833-9800 or write to dublinplazadental@sbcglobal.net. The team will help you directly.
