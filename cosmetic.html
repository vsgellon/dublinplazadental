02.1

Teeth whitening

Two ways to a whiter smile

Whiten in the office in about an hour, or at home over a week or so.

In the office

A concentrated hydrogen peroxide gel is applied two or three times. You are done in about an hour, compared with seven to nine days at home. The enamel is left unharmed and whiter.

Custom trays at home

A moderate peroxide gel goes into trays made for your teeth. Wear them about 30 minutes a day for seven to nine days in a row.

Ready made kits

Preloaded one size trays can also be provided. They make it even simpler to start.

02.2

Bonded veneers

Thin, strong, natural

Veneers are thin custom shells of tooth colored ceramic. They cover at least the whole front of the tooth.

Once bonded in place, veneers are very strong. They can correct teeth that are badly discolored, fractured, or decayed. In some cases they correct crooked teeth too.

02.3

Invisalign

Straighten without metal

Clear, removable aligners move your teeth step by step. Dr. Desai is an Invisalign certified provider.

You switch to a new set of aligners about every week until your teeth reach their final position. They are comfortable and practically unnoticeable.

Take them out to eat, drink, brush, and floss. Invisalign now treats many cases that once needed braces, for teens and adults alike.

02.4

Bonding and white fillings

Small fixes, big difference

Tooth shade ceramic or composite reshapes a tooth or repairs a chip.

Bonding usually conserves natural tooth structure. In some cases it is a good short term alternative to veneers.
