Answers, before you ask

Do you take my insurance?

The office works with most dental plans and PPOs. Call to check your plan. For patients without insurance, the team is happy to discuss ways it might help.

Can I fill out forms before I arrive?

Yes. The secure forms above can be submitted from home before your first appointment.

What happens at the first exam?

Dr. Desai checks the whole mouth, visually and with periodic X rays, and does a simple oral cancer screening. He then explains any treatment options.

Are you open on Saturday?

Saturday is by appointment. Weekday hours are Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, and Friday, 9am to 6pm.

Can I take BART?

Yes. The office is steps from the West Dublin/Pleasanton station. See directions
