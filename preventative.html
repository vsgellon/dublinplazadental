03.1

Comprehensive oral exam

A look at the whole mouth

Your mouth is checked visually and with periodic X rays. Treatment options are recommended only if needed.

Every exam includes a simple oral cancer screening. You also get a review of your home care, with tips where they help.

03.2

Regular cleanings

Clean teeth, healthy gums

Plaque and tartar are removed, leaving teeth smooth and clean. Most surface stains from coffee, tea, and smoking come off too.

For more serious gum conditions, the office offers scaling and root planing, also called a deep cleaning. The team works closely with you to reach a healthier, stable condition you can maintain.

03.3

Digital X rays

See what the eye cannot

X rays help find damage and disease that a visual exam would miss.

The office uses digital imaging, including 2D, bitewing, panoramic, and 3D CBCT. Digital imaging is faster and reduces radiation exposure.

03.4

Sealants

A shield for chewing surfaces

Sealants are thin plastic coatings on the chewing surfaces of permanent teeth. They help prevent cavities.

Sealants are usually placed on children’s teeth.
